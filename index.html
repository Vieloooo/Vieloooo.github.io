I am a PhD student in Financial Technology at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Xuechao Wang and Prof. Yingjie Xue. Previously, I received my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, in 2023.

Research Interests

I study the security and economics of decentralized systems, with a focus on how protocol rules shape incentives and market outcomes.

  • Blockchain economics and mechanism design
  • Payment channels and cross-chain protocols
  • Decentralized finance and market microstructure
